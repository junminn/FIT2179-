Sun Safety: UV Exposure Categories

We should be aware of the uv radiating from the sun. There are a few levels of UV exposure that we should keep an eye out for. Thus, we need to understand the intensity of ultraviolet radiation. Hover over the segments to see safety guidelines and protection requirements for different index levels.

💡 Key Insights

  • The Reflection Effect: UV radiation isn't just direct; it reflects off water, sand, and even pavement, potentially doubling your exposure even in the shade.
  • The Cloud Myth: Up to 80% of UV rays pass through light clouds. Most "overcast" burns happen because people skip protection thinking the sun is blocked.
  • The 10-4 Rule: UV intensity is highest between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM. During these hours, the risk of skin damage is significantly higher regardless of how hot the air feels.
  • Cumulative Damage: Even "Low" and "Moderate" levels contribute to long-term skin aging and damage. Protection should be a daily habit, not just for "Extreme" days.
