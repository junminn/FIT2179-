Malaysia's Weather & Air Quality

Malaysia’s climate is undergoing a significant transformation. As global temperatures rise, the nation faces unique challenges—from intensifying urban heat islands to fluctuating air quality levels. This dashboard explores the intersection of meteorological trends and environmental health, providing a data-driven look at how weather patterns and pollution are shaping the Malaysian landscape today.

Malaysia is heating up!

The map below shows the average surface temperatures across Malaysia. Notice the intense heat concentration in the northern states of the Peninsula compared to the relatively cooler highlands. Moreover, states like Selangor and Kuala Lumpur are among the hottest around the year.

Key Insights

  • Urban Heat: KL and Selangor show significantly higher averages due to urban density.
  • Regional Variance: Northern states like Kedah experience sharper temperature peaks during the dry season.
  • Interactive: Use the slider below the map to filter states by temperature thresholds.

Top 10 Hottest States

While the map provides spatial context, this bar chart allows for a direct comparison of peak temperatures. This helps identify which specific urban centers are facing the most significant heat challenges.
